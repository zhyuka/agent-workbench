# Humanize: formats for specific artifacts

How three kinds of team-facing text are shaped, beyond the patterns in `humanize`. Read on demand when
writing one of them; not loaded every session.

## A team spec

- Ground every element: what it is, what shows, its source as a role, settled or open, and what to
  do. Give it a contents list; mark settled rows green and open ones orange.

## Lines the user will say on a call

- Write them as the user talks: lowercase, short, "i think", often a question. Never a scripted
  "Say: ..." speech.

## A talk or deck

- Show everything real, generalized so any team can map it to its own tools: name the kind of tool a
  team already has (its meeting app's own notes), not one only you use.
